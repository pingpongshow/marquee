| D58 | 2026-10-02 | Seek previews (trickplay) are JPEG sprite sheets of 10×10 tiles, 320 px wide, one tile per 10 s, made from keyframes only (`-skip_frame nokey`, `fps=1/10:round=up`) by a maintenance-window task that stops when the window closes and resumes the next night | Accepted | Keyframe-only decoding keeps it cheap, and NVDEC decoding (with `tonemap_cuda` for HDR) does a 100-minute 4K HDR film in 19 s versus 156 s on the CPU; the CPU path is the fallback. For Apple players (whose AVPlayerViewController only shows thumbnails from HLS I-frame playlists), the same FFmpeg pass also writes an all-keyframe 320-px H.264 MP4 (one frame per 10 s, a fragment each); HLS master playlists advertise it as an `EXT-X-I-FRAME-STREAM-INF` with byte ranges per frame (AVFoundation then reports fast-forward support). Files played by direct play (no HLS) don't get them. |
