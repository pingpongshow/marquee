## Description [4000]

Marquee plays your own media. It connects to a Marquee server you run on your own computer or NAS, and brings your movies, shows, music and recordings to your iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. Marquee doesn't include any media: you need your own server and your own content. The server software is free and open source.

MOVIES AND SHOWS
• Direct play or conversion to suit your device and connection
• Subtitles, audio tracks, chapters and skipping intros and credits
• Continue Watching and recommendations from what you watch
• Watch together with someone else in sync
• AirPlay and Chromecast

MUSIC
• Gapless playback, crossfade, loudness levelling, an equaliser and synced lyrics
• Radios and mixes based on how your music sounds
• Top songs on every artist page, favourites, star ratings and thumbs down
• Share playlists with others on your server
• Lock-screen controls

DOWNLOADS (iPhone and iPad)
• Keep movies, shows and music on your device, at the quality you choose
• An offline library when you can't reach your server
• Changes you make offline sync when you're back

FOR THE HOUSEHOLD
• A profile for each person, with optional PINs
• Kids' profiles with rating and time limits
• Everyone gets their own progress, ratings and suggestions

Requires a Marquee server, which you install yourself. See the website for how.

## App Review notes

Marquee is a client for a self-hosted media server, like other personal media server apps. It includes no media and no content source: every title comes from the user's own server, which they set up with their own files.

To review it, use this demonstration server, which holds only sample media we have the rights to use:

- **Server address:** <DEMO SERVER ADDRESS>
- **Username:** <DEMO USERNAME>
- **Password:** <DEMO PASSWORD>

On the first screen, enter the server address, choose Connect, then sign in.

**App Transport Security:** NSAllowsArbitraryLoads is set because users' own servers are usually reached over plain HTTP on their home network or private VPN, at addresses the app can't know ahead of time. There are no fixed domains to list.

**Local network:** used to find the user's server and Cast devices on their home network.

**Background audio:** used to keep music playing while the app is in the background.

**Accounts:** accounts are created by the person who runs the server, not in the app.
