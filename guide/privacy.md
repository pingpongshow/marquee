# Privacy

Marquee is software you run yourself. There is no Marquee company service, account or cloud behind it, and the people who make Marquee can't see your media, your account or how you use it.

## What the apps do with your data

- **Your own server:** the iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Mac, Android and web apps talk to the Marquee server you connect them to. Your sign-in, library, watch history, ratings, playlists and other activity are stored on that server, under the control of whoever runs it.
- **On your device:** the apps keep your server's address, your sign-in and your settings on the device, plus anything you download. Deleting the app removes them.
- **No tracking:** Marquee's own code has no advertising or analytics, and doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites. The one third-party component that collects anything is Google's Cast SDK, below.

## Other services

Some features contact other services, and only when you use them:

- **Chromecast (iPhone and iPad):** Google's Cast SDK finds and talks to Cast devices on your network. It declares that it collects a device identifier, diagnostics and how the feature is used, not linked to you, for analytics and for making it work. Google describes this in [its privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Trailers:** trailers from YouTube play in an embedded YouTube player, covered by [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Update check (Mac app):** it asks GitHub for the latest release once a day. You can turn this off in Settings.

Whoever runs your server can also turn on features that send information from the server to other services, such as metadata lookups, Live TV sources, requests through Seerr, or In Town, which sends artist names and locations to Ticketmaster. Ask them what's on. Details are in the [guide](README.md).

## Children

A server's administrator can mark profiles as kids' profiles and limit what they can watch. Marquee doesn't collect information from children or anyone else; anything a profile does stays on its server.

## Your choices

Your data lives on the server you use. To see, change or delete it, use the app or ask the person who runs that server. Deleting an account on a server removes its profile and history from that server.
